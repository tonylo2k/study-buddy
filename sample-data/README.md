# Sample data (EXAMPLE ONLY)

These files are **illustrative** Grade 4 elementary content. They are not copied from a specific commercial textbook. Use them to:

1. Seed a “Load sample pack” button in development.
2. Judge whether generated JSON from the live LLM matches this shape and quality.
3. Play the visual explainers in `VisualExplainerPlayer` without calling an API.

Lesson packs:
- `math_grade4_extract.json` + `math_grade4_generated.json` — Math (intro to fractions), including visual flashcards and a visual quiz item
- `social_grade4_extract.json` + `social_grade4_generated.json` — Social Studies (local products and community life), including visual flashcards and visual quiz items

Visual scene specs (animated explainers):
- `visual_weather_vs_climate.json` — Science / social studies contrast (weather = today’s sky changing hour by hour vs. climate = a year of seasons cycling)
- `visual_fractions.json` — Math fraction bars and a number line, matching the math sample pack
- `visual_local_products.json` — Map and process flow matching the social studies sample pack
